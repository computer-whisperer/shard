# The reducer's certificate and the runtime's division — measurement and fix (2026-11-09/10)

A record outside the slice sequence: slice 4.6 was proposed and not ratified when the user asked
for a measurement of what a `tb_len`-class proof costs ("Let's run the proof-cost measurement
first"), then for the fix it pointed at ("Go ahead with the fix test"), then for the landing
("Agreed, let's land this. We should also replace len.shard with the compact version and fix the
reducer's rescan first."). Three changes came of it, each its own commit:

1. `tools/codegen/rt.h` — the machine's division for a dividend within 128 bits over a divisor
   within 64.
2. `v3/kernel/tactic.shard` — `reduce`, `unfold` and `simp_only` show K each step; the scan keeps
   a head constant's facts for the loop; the reconstruction version is 2.
3. `v3/models/imp/len.shard` — the run lemmas run the evaluator; four uncited lemmas dropped.

**Style or gap.** What the tactic layer already did, unused by the file: `(rw fuel_of_succ)`
finds its argument by matching; `(reduce lhs)` runs the evaluator; sixteen straight-line
statements close by fuel rewrites, one `reduce` and `rfl`, no state written, in about a second.
`len_E3b` — three loads, four branches — went from 13,294 bytes of proof to 554 on the first
attempt, the statement's hash and the axioms unchanged. `len_T9_ret` — the call and the overflow
check — did not: the tactics finished in about 6 s and K's check did not end (stopped at 600 s
and 37 GB). Bisected over the statement list: accepted through five statements, no end once the
sixth, a branch whose condition is 64-bit arithmetic over a local, is in the list; unrolling the
fuel in stages changed nothing. A stack sample of the hung loader was in K's `whnf` under
`reduce_nat`, the recursion deep.

**The cause.** `reduce` applied a K-convertible step — a definitional equation, a case step, a
whnf or delta step — by changing the target and making no term, so K, checking the proof, had to
establish that the target before the loop converts to the target after it, by its own reduction.
The reducer waits at an open discriminant (slice 4.2 landing 1, item 3c); K's `whnf` does not,
and at a branch on `(w + (2^64 − 1)) % 2^64` over a local it peels the literal one successor at a
time. That is why slice 4.2 wrote the step laws and every intermediate state: each transition an
equation, no conversion over the evaluator. (Two conjectures of mine about the hang were killed by
test before this one held: a successor-tower fuel at a stuck call, and staging the fuel.)

Gaps met on the way and left: `rw` matches by structure (K accepts `n + 3` for the tower, `rw`
does not); a term has no `_` placeholder; `apply` leaves no argument for a later `rfl` to
determine (`witness_open`).

## The changes

**`reduce`, `unfold`, `simp_only`** (`tactic.shard`: `Evid`, `redex_evidence`, `rewrite_eq`,
`Leg`, `legs_join`, `loop_end`). Every step a motive can abstract carries an equation: the
equation's own proof where one justifies the step (also where that proof is `Eq.refl`), `Eq.refl`
at the subterm where the step is K's. The loop's equations are composed as a balanced tree of
`Eq.trans` under one `Eq.mpr`: the first form, an `Eq.mpr` nested per step, ended the hang and K
refused it `exhausted_rec_depth_at_infer` on a run of a few statements. A step under a binder, or
under a dependency, stays by conversion where it is K-convertible — no motive abstracts it.

**The scan** (`HeadFacts`, `head_of`). `find_redex` asked `equations_of` at every subterm on every
step — `f.eq_k` built and looked up, and for a head with none, every constructor, `M.f.realize_k`
for the module and each one it opens — and asked whether a subterm is a proposition before asking
whether its head reduces. A head's equations and reducibility are now found once per loop, and the
type of a constructor's application is not inferred. (I had called this "the rescan"; the profile
says the scan is cheap and what it asked at each subterm was not.)

**The reconstruction version** is 2 (law §7.2; slice 4.5 rule 2): the same I elaborates to another
P. Against a store written before the change, `--reconstruct` over `len`'s closure: 421 theorems,
381 the same, 40 an evidence change (step 14, len 13, std.map 4, word 4, cell 4, std.list 1). The
nine sidecars carry `(sidecar 3 2)` — the form's line alone, no entry re-keyed —; `store_test`,
`tactic_lib_test` and the pin `auto_recon_old` name the current version.

**The runtime's division** (`tools/codegen/rt.h`, `rt_idiv2`). The profile of the loader, on any
file: half of the samples in name and node hashing. `mix a b` is `(a · 1000003 + b + 1) mod (2^61 −
1)`: the product leaves the small integers, and the `mod` was a bit-serial division of a 96-bit
dividend ("division is rare in cert checking", true of the old tree). A dividend within 128 bits
over a divisor within 64 now takes the machine's division on the magnitudes, the signs applied as
before. Checked against `rt_mag_divmod` on 25,729,927 random magnitudes with edge limbs, 0
mismatches; the loader's output byte-identical on three loads.

**`len.shard`.** The seven run lemmas `tb_len_aux` cites run the evaluator: the fuel's levels
turned to `FS` by `fuel_of_succ` as far as the run is to go, `reduce`, and where the run waits the
hypothesis rewritten in. `len_S1`, `len_S2`, `len_S2_one` and `len_E3a` served only the other
lemmas' old proofs and are gone. Every remaining statement keeps its hash; `tb_len` rests on Init's
three axioms. `tb_len_aux` (35,060 bytes) and `app.shard` are as they were.

## The third round — `mix` in pieces under 63 bits (2026-11-10)

The user, on the proposal the measurement ended in: "go ahead with step 1" — the cheaper `mix`.
Steps 2 and 3 of that proposal (a proof admitted with its sharing; the scan's matcher asked once)

**The change.** `kernel/name.shard`: `mix a b` is `(a · 1000003 + b + 1) mod M`, M = 2^61 − 1. For
`a` in [0, 2^61) — a hash or a tag, every caller's — the product is now taken in pieces: with
`a = a1 · 2^31 + a0` and `a1 · 1000003 = u1 · 2^30 + u0`, `a · 1000003 = u1 · 2^61 + u0 · 2^31 +
a0 · 1000003`, and 2^61 is 1 modulo M; no intermediate passes 2^62, so none is a bignum. Any other
`a` takes the formula written whole (`mix_whole`). The value is the same for every input (`mod` is
the Euclidean one, so `b` is free). What says so: the algebra, run in exact arithmetic on two
million inputs (a scratch script, not kept); four cases in `kernel/test/expr_test.shard` — two
values by hand, the formula against `mix` over the range's ends, both sides of each split, outside
the range and with `b` past 64 and 128 bits and negative, and along a walk of 2,000 hashes; the
compiled loader's output byte-identical before and after on `len.shard`, `app.shard` and the two
together, and the bootstrap's on `len.shard` to its last definition; `suite_tie`; and T0, whose
pins are hashes.
