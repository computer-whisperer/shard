## 4. What memoization can and cannot reach

- CAN reach: re-normalization of shared subterms inside a claim — the
  late-fold walks; a slice of env_lookup traffic attributable to
  re-evaluating telescoped values. Addressable pool at full-chain
  extrapolation: a fraction of §3's 170–400M.
- CANNOT reach: the load floor — parse (317M), name resolution
  (~85M), type-gate substitution (58M), env traffic from FIRST
  evaluations. That is attacked by (i) text shrink, already banked
  10–25x on the exercised family with the naive forms, and (ii) §7
  storage (binary/DAG serialization, parse once, content addressing).
- Independent bound: at today's closures the entire rewrite/eval
  machinery is ~2–3M calls per fn — even ZEROING proof-step compute
  moves nothing visible against the 555M+ parse+env share.
- The A2 structural finding stands: naming ladders (the gb_/xv
  discipline the generator already practices) bound the telescope BY
  CONSTRUCTION. Every improvement in naming discipline shrinks the
  memo's addressable pool further.

## 5. The forks, priced

**A — targeted compute memo in the checker** (semantics-free caching of
compute_eval at call-headed named fns, keyed by (fn, args, stop set)).
Bill: state threading through the normalizer in kernel shard source —
both engines compile FROM those sources, no native-only shortcut; key
equality needs deep term compare (expensive WITHOUT interning — the
memo wants the arena anyway); verdict-identity obligation (caching must
be observationally invisible, its own pin family); stop-set keying
subtlety. Days-scale, kernel-touching. Payoff: a fraction of §3's
extrapolated 170–400M, only realized at full-chain scale we have not
built, on a cost axis (calls) the wall clock doesn't currently feel.

**B — hash-consed term arena** (interning at read; id equality; memo
becomes cheap; nodes content-addressed). This is the real substrate —
and it is ALREADY §7's slice: DAG storage, binary serialization,
content-addressed certs, and gate (d) incremental recheck all want the
same arena. Bill: term representation threads reader/types/checker
(canon-owned files), arena state in a pure-functional kernel — the
deepest commitment in the whole redirection. Building it as "rung (e)"
would ratify §7's core design as a side effect of a rung gated on
marginals that don't demand it.

**C — defer: record the A3 verdict on rungs (a)–(d); sharing rides §7.**
Rationale: the naive forms already beat patch 3.6x on marginal calls
and 10–25x on text with the kernel bill fully paid (one sort gate, two
search-free forms, zero axioms); the recompute is ≤0.65% at exercised
scale and plausibly nets NEGATIVE at full scale against the replay text
it deletes; the dominant measured cost (load floor) is §7's territory,
where the arena lands ONCE serving storage + memo + incremental
together, priced as a slice with its own gates instead of arriving
sideways through a rung.

**D — measure the replacement basis first**: re-derive a 3-seam sample
(or the full 13-seam chain) of the block walk in the conversion
dialect, WITHOUT the cmp_ family in the closure, SHARD_STATS both ways.
Turns §3's extrapolation + the unknown deletion credit into one number.
No kernel bill; proof-authoring days on the xconv patterns we own. Also
the evidence DC3 (checkpointed-walk form) is explicitly waiting on
("generated walks still too big after conversion+DAG?").

## 6. Recommendation

**C now; D folded into the next block-chain touch** (the deferred full
walk re-derivation, or the coverage compiler's first family — which
must be born speaking this dialect anyway and will produce D's number
as a by-product of its own gate). Do not build A: bounded payoff, real
kernel bill, and its useful half (the arena) is B. Do not build B as a
rung: it is §7's slice and should be priced there with serialization +
content addressing + gate (d) incremental in one design, on D's number.

The A3 verdict then records: rungs (a)–(d) landed and measured; rung
(e) resolved by DESCOPE-TO-§7 with this memo as the evidence trail;
gate (b) status = text 10–25x + marginal calls 3.6x on the exercised
family, replacement-basis number owed by D; gate (d) explicitly
deferred to §7. DC2 (surface spelling) comes up for final adjudication
with the verdict, per §11.
